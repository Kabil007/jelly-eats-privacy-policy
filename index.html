Privacy Policy

Jelly Eats: Maze Adventure

Effective date: September 28, 2026

F11 Software Lab ("we", "our", or "us") operates Jelly Eats: Maze Adventure ("the Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

The current version of Jelly Eats does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address in order to play.

2. Game Progress and Local Data

Jelly Eats stores certain game information locally on your device, including game progress, completed levels, stars, coins, selected characters, unlocked cosmetic content, and game settings.

This information is used to provide the Game's functionality and preserve your progress. In the current version, this game progress is stored locally on your device.

3. Advertising

The initial release of Jelly Eats does not display advertising. Advertising functionality may be introduced in a future version.

If advertising is enabled in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate to explain the advertising services used and how information may be processed by those services.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

The initial release of Jelly Eats does not offer active paid subscriptions or in-app purchases.

If purchases or subscriptions are introduced in a future version, payment transactions will be processed through the applicable app store, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store. This Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate when those features become available.

5. Device Permissions

Jelly Eats is designed to request only permissions necessary for its functionality. The Game does not intentionally request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location for normal gameplay.

6. Third-Party Services

The current release is designed so that advertising and paid subscription services are disabled. Future versions may integrate third-party services when additional features are introduced.

When such services become active, this Privacy Policy may be updated to identify those services and provide relevant information about their privacy practices.

7. Children's Privacy

Jelly Eats does not knowingly request or collect personal information directly from children through account registration or user-submitted profile information.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that personal information has been provided to us through the Game, please contact us using the details below.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and settings stored locally remain on your device until they are cleared through the Game or device, or the Game is uninstalled, depending on the device and operating-system behavior.

Because the current version does not require an online Jelly Eats account, there is no online Jelly Eats account data for us to delete.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to design and maintain the Game with privacy and security in mind. However, no electronic system or method of storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game changes, including when new services such as advertising, purchases, subscriptions, or online features are introduced.

Updates will be posted on this page and the effective date will be revised when appropriate.
